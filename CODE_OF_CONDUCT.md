# Code of Conduct

## The short version

This is a project for helping people learn. Assume good faith, be patient with
beginners, and criticise ideas rather than people.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be welcoming. "This is a basic question" is never a reason not to answer it.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. "This function double-negates the
  cost matrix" is useful; "this is sloppy" is not.
- Accept correction gracefully. Being wrong about a Nash equilibrium is normal;
  that is why the tests exist.
- Respect that people contribute in their spare time.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Deliberately derailing discussions or dismissing newcomers
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional or academic setting

## Academic integrity

gtlab exists to help people *understand* game theory. Using it to check your
reasoning, explore parameters, or see why an answer is what it is — that is
exactly the point. Submitting its output as your own coursework, where your
institution forbids that, is on you.

Please do not open issues or pull requests asking for solutions to a live
assignment, and please do not upload copyrighted course material (lecture
slides, problem sheets, solution manuals) to this repository.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests,
discussions, and commit messages — and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/DataAthleteChamp/gam_theory_vis/security/advisories/new)
(it is private, and works fine for conduct reports) or by contacting the
maintainer [@DataAthleteChamp](https://github.com/DataAthleteChamp).

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The
maintainer is obligated to respect the privacy and security of anyone who
reports an incident.

Maintainers may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban any contributor for
behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful, and
may remove comments, commits, code, or issues that violate this Code of Conduct.
